🔑 Key Insight

Why chunks instead of whole documents?

Breaking documents into 1000-character chunks allows for precise retrieval of relevant sections, not entire documents. This means when you ask "What was Q3 revenue?", the system finds the specific paragraph about Q3 revenue, not the entire 50-page annual report.

💡 Key Concepts You Implemented

1. Vector Embeddings - The Core of RAG

What are they? Text converted into numbers (vectors) that represent meaning.

graph LR A["Text: 'Revenue increased 20%'"] --> B[Embedding Model] B --> C["Vector: [0.23, -0.45, 0.67, ...]
384 dimensions"] D["Question: 'What was revenue growth?'"] --> B B --> E["Vector: [0.25, -0.43, 0.69, ...]"] C --> F{Cosine Similarity} E --> F F --> G["Score: 0.92
VERY SIMILAR!"] style G fill:#4caf50,color:#fff

Why it matters: Similar meanings produce similar vectors, enabling semantic search without exact keyword matching.
